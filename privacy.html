BrightDesk – Privacy Policy

Publisher: Nez Apps · Last updated: 25 November 2026

BrightDesk is a Windows app that adjusts the brightness of your laptop screen and external monitors. This policy explains what information BrightDesk handles. The short version: BrightDesk does not collect, store, sell or share any personal information.

Information we collect

None. BrightDesk has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. It does not send any information about you, your computer or how you use the app to Nez Apps or anyone else.

Information stored on your device

BrightDesk saves your preferences (for example: brightness schedule times, keyboard shortcut settings and per-screen adjustments) in a small settings file on your own computer, in your Windows app data folder. This file never leaves your device, and it is removed when you uninstall the app.

To control external monitors, BrightDesk reads each monitor's model name and current settings (brightness, and for Pro also contrast, volume and input) through the monitor cable (DDC/CI). This is used only to show and change those settings, and it stays on your computer.

Purchases

BrightDesk is free. The optional BrightDesk Pro upgrade is a one-time purchase handled entirely by the Microsoft Store. BrightDesk only asks Windows whether Pro has been purchased, and remembers that answer on your computer so Pro keeps working offline; it never sees your payment details. Microsoft's handling of your purchase is covered by the Microsoft Privacy Statement.

Children

BrightDesk does not collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
